**Full description:**
Pour, sort, relax. Sand Jars is a calm colour-sorting puzzle with real falling sand.

Tap a jar to pick it up, then tap another to pour. Sand only lands on the same colour or in an empty jar. Sort every jar into a single colour to finish the level. Every grain streams out of the glass and piles up in soft, wavy layers, just like a sand-art bottle.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Real sand: every scoop is hundreds of grains that pour, tumble and settle
• Soothing sounds: the hiss of falling sand and the pop of a cork when a jar is done
• Souvenir bottles: every level adds a layer to a sand-art bottle. Finish it and it goes on your shelf. 20 designs to collect
• Fresh twists: narrow-neck jars, covered scoops and sticky wet sand
• Hard levels every ten levels for double coins
• A new Daily Jar every day, with a streak to keep
• Undo, Add a jar and Hint boosters when you're stuck
• Jar styles: Mason, Spice, Wax seal, Crystal and Gold rim
• No timers, no pressure. Play offline at your own pace
